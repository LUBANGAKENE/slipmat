How it works

01

Photograph the sleeve

Front cover, back cover, or centre label. A vision model transcribes what's printed and says so plainly when something's illegible, rather than guessing.

02

Your pressing decides

The back cover's tracklist wins over any database. Discogs and MusicBrainz only fill in when no tracklist is visible — and prefer the vinyl pressing over a CD reissue, so you get A1/B2, not 1, 2, 3.

03

BPM and key, resolved

Matched against ReccoBeats, with an optional Spotify search hop to sharpen the match. Camelot notation, half/double-time candidates, pitch-fader math included.

04

Save it, browse it

Sign in — optional, your own Supabase project — and keep a library: a cover-art grid, browse by artist, or a flat sortable Rekordbox-style track list.
