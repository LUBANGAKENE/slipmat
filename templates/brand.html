Construction
  • Disc diameter = 12 units. Label = 5.75 units, centred.
  • Spindle hole = 1 unit, always visible.
  • Clear space on all sides = 1.5 units (label radius ÷ 2).
  • Minimum size: 24px digital, 10mm print — drop the grooves below 32px.
Don't
  • Don't rotate the wordmark inside the label — it stays horizontal even when the disc spins.
  • Don't recolour the label anything but Signal Red, ink or paper.
  • Don't add a glow, bevel or motion blur to the disc.
  • Don't place the mark over a photo without the ink scrim.
